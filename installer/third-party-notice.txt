Jalyro Convert — third-party components
================================================================

Jalyro Convert itself is MIT licensed. It includes and runs the following
components under their own licences.

FFmpeg — GPL version 2 or later
----------------------------------------------------------------
Used for all audio and video conversion, and for decoding HEIC images.

FFmpeg is included as a separate executable and is invoked over its documented
command-line interface. Its full licence text is installed alongside it, in the
"ffmpeg" folder inside the installation directory.

The corresponding source code for the FFmpeg build included here is linked from
the release page this installer came from.

  https://ffmpeg.org
  https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

libvips — LGPL version 2.1 or later
----------------------------------------------------------------
Used for all still-image conversion.

  https://libvips.org

Patent notice
----------------------------------------------------------------
H.264 and H.265 video carry patent obligations that are separate from software
licensing, and vary by jurisdiction and use. This is the same position as other
free FFmpeg-based tools.
