What it does

Select one file or two hundred, right-click, pick a format. The converted files appear beside the originals. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

ImagesJPG · PNG · WEBP · AVIF · TIFF — in and out. HEIC, HEIF, BMP and GIF in.
VideoMP4 · WEBM out. MOV, MKV, AVI, WMV, FLV and more in.
AudioMP3 · WAV · FLAC · M4A · OPUS, and extract audio from any video.

HEIC works without buying a codec

iPhone photos are HEIC, and Windows normally asks you to buy the HEVC extension from the Microsoft Store to open them. Jalyro Convert decodes HEIC itself. Nothing to buy, nothing to install.

It preserves quality

Most converters re-encode at a fixed quality, which either bloats an already-compressed file or throws away detail from a good one. This one reads what the source was saved at and matches it. Lossless formats stay lossless: PNG to WEBP discards nothing at all.

Compression happens only when you ask for it — Compress for email and Discord-friendly work backwards from a real size limit rather than guessing at settings.

It stays out of the way

  • Appears in the main Windows 11 menu, not under “Show more options”.
  • One image converts silently — no window, no notification.
  • Batches show progress and can be cancelled.
  • Never overwrites anything. Collisions become photo (1).jpg.
  • Runs the conversion outside Explorer, so a broken file can’t take your desktop down.
